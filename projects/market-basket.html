BUSINESS PROBLEM

Which products should be considered together?

The project starts from a practical commercial question: products do not behave independently. If customers repeatedly purchase certain items together, those relationships can inform bundle offers, product placement and promotional decisions.

The README frames three uses for the analysis: smarter promotions, strategic placement, and understanding which customers repeatedly buy related products. citeturn0view0

DATA & PREPARATION

From invoice rows to shopping baskets.

The notebook reads fake_sales.xlsx and works with fields including InvoiceID, Date, CustomerNo, ItemID, Qty, Value, NetValue, promotion value and warehouse information.

A discount-percentage field is derived, then the sales table is pivoted by InvoiceID and ItemID. Each basket becomes a binary representation: 1 when a product appears in the invoice and 0 otherwise. The resulting matrix is 198 × 100. citeturn1view0

ANALYTICAL PIPELINE

A clear path from transactions to recommendations.

The notebook explores support/confidence combinations before applying Apriori with a 1% minimum support and a maximum itemset length of six, followed by association rules with a 0.20 minimum confidence threshold. citeturn2view0turn3view0

01 · PrepareClean sales data and create the invoice × product basket.
02 · DiscoverUse Apriori to identify frequent itemsets.
03 · MeasureEvaluate support, confidence, lift and related rule metrics.
04 · ActivateUse product relationships to inform bundles, placement and promotions.

KEY ANALYTICAL FINDINGS

Not every frequent combination is a meaningful relationship.

The notebook explicitly evaluates support, confidence and lift rather than relying only on raw co-occurrence. The generated rules table contains 663 rules and 16 columns, while the top-lift output reaches 99.0 for several sparse combinations. citeturn3view1turn2view3

Example rule

Product 100000 → Product 100048
Support: 1.52% · Confidence: 30% · Lift: 4.95×

This illustrates why lift is useful: a relationship can be relatively uncommon overall while still being substantially stronger than would be expected from the products' individual popularity. citeturn3view0turn2view3

THE CUSTOM RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

bst_products() turns rules into product recommendations.

Instead of leaving the output as a large association-rules table, the notebook builds a reusable function that accepts a Product ID and filters the rules to find relevant associated products.

It calculates shared invoices, shared invoices per month, confidence percentage and the number of unique customers overlapping between the target and recommended products. The final result is narrowed to the strongest recommendations. citeturn3view2turn3view3

BUSINESS APPLICATION

The output is designed to answer a commercial question.

For a selected product, the analysis can surface the products most closely associated with it and provide additional context around how often the products appear together and how many customers overlap. That creates a practical foundation for bundle design, shelf/catalogue placement, cross-selling and promotional targeting. citeturn0view0turn3view2
