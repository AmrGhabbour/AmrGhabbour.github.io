PYTHON · MACHINE LEARNING · CUSTOMER ANALYTICS

Telecom Customer
Churn Prediction

An end-to-end customer analytics and machine learning project designed to identify customers at risk of churn and prioritize them for targeted retention actions.

Python Pandas Scikit-learn XGBoost EDA Decile Analysis

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Who is most likely to leave?

Customer churn is a major challenge for telecom companies. Instead of treating every customer equally, the business needs to identify customers who are most likely to churn and focus retention resources where they can have the greatest impact.

BUSINESS QUESTION

Where should retention teams focus?

The objective was not simply to maximize prediction accuracy. The project focused on identifying meaningful churn drivers, predicting customer-level churn risk, and ranking customers according to their probability of leaving.

DATASET

7,043 customers · 21 variables

The dataset contains customer demographics, telecom services, contract information, payment methods, tenure and customer charges. The target variable is whether the customer churned.

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

From customer behavior to retention strategy.

01

Data Understanding

Explored customer characteristics, variable types, distributions and the overall churn profile.

02

Exploratory Analysis

Investigated relationships between customer attributes, services, contracts, payment methods and churn.

03

Feature Engineering

Converted categorical variables into predictive features, standardized numerical variables and removed identifiers.

04

Machine Learning

Compared Random Forest, XGBoost and Logistic Regression using multiple classification metrics.

05

Model Evaluation

Evaluated accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score and confusion matrices with particular attention to recall.

06

Decile Analysis

Ranked customers by predicted churn probability and segmented them into ten risk groups for retention prioritization.

KEY INSIGHTS

Churn is not evenly distributed across customers.

Month-to-Month Contracts

Customers on month-to-month contracts showed a strong association with churn, making contract migration a potential retention opportunity.

Short Tenure

Customers with shorter relationships were more likely to churn, suggesting the importance of early-life retention and onboarding programs.

Fiber Optic Customers

Fiber Optic customers demonstrated higher churn levels, creating an opportunity to investigate pricing, service quality and customer expectations.

Electronic Check

Electronic check users showed higher churn, suggesting potential opportunities around payment experience and automated payment adoption.

MODEL RESULTS

Why XGBoost?

Logistic Regression achieved the highest overall accuracy, but accuracy alone was not the right business objective. For churn prediction, failing to identify a customer who will actually leave can be more costly than targeting a customer who ultimately stays.

DECILE ANALYSIS

Prediction is only the first step.

Customers were ranked according to their predicted churn probability and divided into ten deciles. This transforms the model from a prediction engine into a prioritization framework for customer retention.

The analysis suggests focusing retention efforts on approximately the first five deciles, where the cumulative recall provides the strongest opportunity before additional targeting produces diminishing returns.

BUSINESS APPLICATION

Turning churn probability into retention action.

HIGH RISK

Immediate Intervention

Target customers with retention offers, personalized plans, contract incentives and proactive support.

MEDIUM RISK

Personalized Engagement

Use targeted communication, loyalty incentives and relevant service recommendations.

LOW RISK

Maintain Relationship

Continue standard customer communication and loyalty programs without unnecessary retention spend.

PROJECT TAKEAWAY

A good churn model doesn't just predict who will leave. It helps the business decide who to act on first.

By combining exploratory analysis, predictive modeling, XGBoost and decile analysis, this project demonstrates how customer analytics can move from understanding behavior to prioritizing real-world retention decisions.
