BUSINESS PROBLEM

What should the business understand?

Sales teams can generate large amounts of transactional data, but raw figures alone do not explain why performance changes. A useful reporting solution brings key measures into one view and makes it easier to investigate differences across time, customers, products, and sales representatives.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how a structured BI report can support performance monitoring and help stakeholders move from observing results to asking better business questions.

ANALYTICAL QUESTIONS

Questions worth investigating.

  • How is revenue changing over time?
  • Which products or customer groups contribute most to sales?
  • How does sales performance vary across representatives or regions?
  • Where are the largest positive or negative changes occurring?
  • Which areas deserve further investigation or action?

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

A repeatable path from data to insight.

The workflow below describes a typical BI process. Keep only the steps that reflect what you actually performed in this project.

  • 01 Understand the business requirements Identify stakeholder questions, reporting needs, business definitions, and the decisions the analysis should support.
  • 02 Prepare and validate the data Inspect data types, missing values, duplicate records, date fields, and consistency in key business measures.
  • 03 Build the analytical model Structure the data and define relationships and calculations required for reliable reporting.
  • 04 Develop the reporting layer Use appropriate measures, visuals, filters, and drill-downs to make performance easier to explore.
  • 05 Interpret findings and recommend actions Validate important patterns, explain their business relevance, and distinguish measured results from recommendations that require further testing.

BUSINESS VALUE

The goal is not another dashboard. It is a better decision.

A well-designed sales reporting solution can help teams monitor performance consistently, identify meaningful changes earlier, and focus investigation on the products, customers, periods, or sales activities that matter most.

The specific findings, recommendations, and quantified business impact should be added after validating the actual report results.
