PYTHON · GEOSPATIAL ANALYTICS · HEALTHCARE

Kuwait Pharmacies
Accessibility Analysis

A geospatial healthcare analysis designed to identify pharmacy accessibility gaps across Kuwait by combining pharmacy locations, population density, neighborhood boundaries and commercial activity.

Python GeoPandas OSMnx Folium Pandas Spatial Analysis Location Intelligence

BUSINESS PROBLEM

Where are healthcare access gaps?

A high number of pharmacies at the national level does not necessarily mean that healthcare access is evenly distributed. The objective was to understand how pharmacy locations align with population distribution and identify areas where large populations may have limited access to pharmaceutical services.

BUSINESS QUESTION

Where should the next pharmacy be?

The analysis goes beyond simply mapping pharmacies. By combining population density, service coverage and commercial activity, it creates a location-intelligence framework that can support pharmacy expansion and healthcare accessibility decisions.

DATA SOURCES

Combining geography with demographics.

The project integrates multiple geographic and demographic datasets to create a unified view of healthcare accessibility across Kuwait.

OPENSTREETMAP

Pharmacy & Commercial Locations

Pharmacy, chemist and commercial facility locations retrieved through OSMnx and OpenStreetMap.

PACI

Official Neighborhood Boundaries

Official Kuwaiti neighborhood boundaries used to aggregate pharmacy counts and population relationships.

KONTUR

Population Grid

High-resolution 400m population data used to identify densely populated areas with insufficient pharmacy coverage.

LOCAL GIS DATA

Healthcare & Road Networks

Additional geographic layers used to support healthcare facility analysis and spatial interpretation.

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

From raw coordinates to location intelligence.

01

Geospatial Data Acquisition

Retrieved pharmacy and commercial facility locations across Kuwait using OpenStreetMap and OSMnx.

02

Coordinate Standardization

Standardized geographic data into UTM Zone 39N (EPSG:32639) to enable accurate metric-based spatial calculations.

03

Neighborhood Aggregation

Used spatial joins to count pharmacies within official PACI neighborhood boundaries.

04

Service Area Modeling

Created a 1 km service-area buffer around pharmacy locations to approximate accessible coverage.

05

Pharmacy Desert Detection

Identified population cells containing more than 5,000 residents that fall outside the modeled 1 km pharmacy service area.

06

Market Intelligence

Analyzed commercial brands and amenities to identify high-traffic business hubs that could support future healthcare locations.

KEY FINDINGS

Pharmacy availability is a location problem, not simply a pharmacy-count problem.

10,000+ Residents per Pharmacy

Several neighborhoods showed population-to-pharmacy ratios exceeding 10,000 residents per pharmacy, highlighting potential service pressure.

High-Density Pharmacy Deserts

Population-dense residential areas were identified where residents fall outside the modeled 1 km pharmacy service range.

Population Changes the Story

Mapping pharmacies alone can hide underserved communities. Overlaying population density reveals where access gaps matter most.

Commercial Hubs Create Opportunities

Commercial brand concentration can provide an additional signal for identifying high-activity areas that may support new pharmacy locations.

LOCATION INTELLIGENCE

Three metrics turn maps into decisions.

01

Population / Pharmacy

Measures the number of residents served by each pharmacy within a neighborhood.

02

Service Coverage

Measures which population areas fall within or outside the 1 km pharmacy accessibility zone.

03

Commercial Activity

Uses commercial amenity concentration as an additional signal for potential location attractiveness.

BUSINESS APPLICATION

From “Where are pharmacies?” to “Where should we invest next?”

HEALTHCARE PLANNING

Improve Accessibility

Prioritize areas where population density is high but pharmacy coverage is limited.

EXPANSION

Identify New Locations

Combine underserved population clusters with commercial activity to generate potential expansion candidates.

MARKET STRATEGY

Support Location Decisions

Give healthcare and retail decision-makers a geographic evidence layer for evaluating future investments.

ANALYTICAL VALUE

Geography becomes a business variable.

This project demonstrates how spatial data can be integrated with demographic and commercial information to answer business questions that traditional tabular analysis cannot answer on its own.

Instead of asking only how many pharmacies exist, the analysis asks where they are, who they serve, who remains outside their coverage, and what other signals indicate a potentially attractive location.

PROJECT TAKEAWAY

The best location is not necessarily where demand is highest. It is where demand, accessibility and market opportunity intersect.

By combining geospatial analysis, population data and commercial intelligence, this project creates a practical framework for identifying healthcare accessibility gaps and supporting location-based business decisions.
